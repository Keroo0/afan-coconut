# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Affan Coconut serves a mixed local audience: nearby residents, students, workers, and passing riders who want an affordable, refreshing drink. Most visitors will encounter the site on a mobile phone and need to understand the product, price, location, and ordering path quickly.

## Product Purpose

The landing page introduces Affan Coconut, makes its coconut drinks look fresh and desirable, helps visitors compare the menu, and turns interest into a visit or WhatsApp enquiry. Success means visitors immediately understand what is sold, feel tempted by the product imagery, and can find or contact the stall without friction.

## Brand Personality

Cheerful, refreshing, and down-to-earth. The voice is friendly Indonesian: concise, inviting, and lightly playful without sounding forced or overly promotional.

## Anti-references

The site must not look luxurious, formal, corporate, template-driven, or obviously AI-generated. Avoid glassmorphism, generic rounded card grids, empty decorative gradients, repeated section eyebrows, exaggerated marketing claims, crude sketch illustrations, and visual effects unrelated to coconut freshness.

## Design Principles

1. Make the drink desirable before explaining it.
2. Keep prices, location, opening hours, and WhatsApp effortless to scan.
3. Use real product texture and coconut-water freshness as the visual world.
4. Let playful energy support the product rather than overpower it.
5. Design mobile-first for customers making quick decisions while on the move.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard focus, semantic structure, and touch targets. Respect reduced-motion preferences, provide useful alt text for product photography, and do not rely on color alone to communicate meaning.
